Books
The Ground Above The Stars Once, the great Realm of Earth was awash in magic. Now matter is drained of the ether that made marvels. Or so insists Cara, rogue queen of Astral who has observed growing power used for ill in the Eight Realms, corrupting the natural chaos of Ground that supports them all. Eight human allies will help her mend Ground in the Realms—if they can contain their own jealousies and fears, their own temptations to seize a kingdom, and instead defend their home and each other.
The New Leaf Altar For centuries, on behalf of the Eight Realms, Omnis Dominum has proudly protected Earth from the scourge of magic. But during his most recent mission, he’s not so sure the other Realms have Earth’s interests at heart. How will this guardian honor the truth yet keep his promises?
