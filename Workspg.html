Books

'The Ground Above the Stars'

Two cynical children of a failed parapsychologist discover that Earth is just one of the Eight Realms, kingdoms on the eve of declaring war. As the conflict undermines physical laws and brings the threat of otherworldly invaders, the two defend the home they love and each other.

'The New Leaf Altar'

Fow centuries, on behalf of the Eight Realms, Omnis Dominum has proudly protected Earth from the scourge of magic. But during his most recent mission, he’s not so sure the other Realms have Earth’s interests at heart. How will this guardian honor the truth yet keep his promises?
