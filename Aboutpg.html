About
T. Kamman is a developmental psychologist who lives in Florence, South Carolina. Originally from St. Paul, Minnesota, her heart was transplanted to the West, where she got her doctorate at the University of Montana and her education in life in the Big Horn mountains of Wyoming. Coming to the South to teach and do research at Francis Marion University, she found herself in an alien realm, a perfect place to wander among the feral cats, writing spiders and rat snakes in her garden and write contemporary paranormal fantasy—always with a nod to science. If she had ever been initiated into a secret society, it would have been long, long ago and she wouldn’t tell about it here. The Ground Above the Stars is her first completed work of fiction.
